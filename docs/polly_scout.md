# Polly Scout

Polly Scout helps you discover datasets across your research universe through natural language queries. Submit a query in plain English, review the AI-extracted intent, and explore live results as they stream in.

## 1. Getting Started: Authentication

To use Scout, sign in to your Polly account. Scout automatically uses your Polly session, so no separate login is required.

1. **Sign in to Polly**: Open the Polly platform and sign in to your account.

2. **Launch Scout**: Navigate to Scout from the Polly applications menu.

3. **Get started**: You will be taken directly to the Scout Home Dashboard.

![Sign in to Scout](../img/Polly-Scout/1.Login.png) <center>Sign in to continue</center>

## 2. Submit a Query

### Step 1: Write Your Natural Language Query

1. **Enter your query** in plain English. For example: *"Human liver cancer RNA-seq studies with at least 50 samples"*.

2. **Select a source** from the dropdown (GEO, PubMed, ClinicalTrials, or other available sources).

3. **Click "Extract Intent"** to submit. Scout immediately begins extracting structured intent from your query.

![Submit a query](../img/Submit_query.png) <center>Submit your query</center>

### Viewing Past Queries

- The **Home Dashboard** lists all your recent queries with status badges and timestamps.

- Click any row to open its **Query Overview** page.

- Scout runs searches in the background, so you never have to wait on a loading screen.

## 3. Review & Approve Intent

After submitting a query, Scout's AI analyzes your natural language input and extracts structured intent criteria such as organisms, diseases, assay types, and more.

### Step 2: Review Extracted Intent

The Intent Review page displays the following criteria pulled from your query:

- **Organisms**: Species mentioned in your query (e.g., *Homo sapiens*, *Mus musculus*).

- **Diseases**: Conditions or pathologies extracted from your query.

- **Assay Types**: Experimental methods (e.g., RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq).

- **Data Modalities**: Types of data to be matched.

- **Cell Types, Tissues, Genes, Treatments, Platforms**: Additional biological context.

![Review extracted intent](../img/3.Review_approve.png) <center>Review the extracted intent criteria</center>

### Step 3: Approve to Generate Results

1. **Review** all extracted intent criteria on the Intent Review page.

2. (Optional) Click **"Re-extract"** if you want Scout to analyze your query again after editing.

3. **Click "Start Search"** to confirm and begin the results pipeline.

Once approved, Scout starts full AI scoring. Results will begin streaming in shortly.

## 4. Live Results & Exploration

### Step 4: Explore Results as They Stream In

After approving intent, you will be taken to the Results page.

- **Datasets appear live** as the AI pipeline scores them. You do not need to wait for all results to load.

- A **Progress Timeline** at the top tracks the current pipeline stage.

- Click **"Watch Live"** from the Query Overview page to return to an in-progress search.

![Generating results](../img/4.Live_Results1.png) <center>AI agents running the query</center>

![Streaming results](../img/4.Live_Results2.png) <center>Datasets stream in as they are scored</center>

### Results Table Controls

- **Column Picker**: Click **"Columns"** to toggle metadata fields on or off. The list is scrollable when many columns are available.

- **Sorting**: Click any column header to sort datasets.

- **Fetch More Datasets**: Click **"Fetch More"** to load the next batch of scored datasets, when available.

- **CSV Export**: Click **"CSV"** to download the current filtered view.

![Fetch more datasets](../img/4.Live_Results3.png) <center>Load more datasets</center>

### Cancelling a Search

You can stop a search at any time while results are being generated by clicking **"Cancel Search"**. Please note:

- The query immediately stops processing additional datasets, regardless of how many were available (for example, if 800 datasets could have been processed, no further datasets will be scored).

- Only the datasets already loaded at the time of cancellation will be shown.

- The view transforms to display these partial results as the final set for that query.

### Completed Results View

Once all datasets are scored and the search is complete, the Results page displays the full set with all metadata columns populated:

![Completed results](../img/4.Live_Results4.png) <center>Completed results view</center>

### Navigation

- Click **"View Query"** to return to the Query Overview page.

- Click **"New Search"** to start a fresh query from the Home Dashboard.

## 5. Query Overview Page

Every query has a dedicated overview page accessible from the Home Dashboard. It shows the current status, action banners, and the extracted intent.

### Status Banners

Each query displays a status banner with a corresponding action:

- **Awaiting Approval**: Intent has been extracted. Click **"Review Intent"** to proceed.

- **Generating Results**: Pipeline is running. Click **"Watch Live"** to monitor progress.

- **Results Ready**: Click **"View Results"** to open the Results page.

- **No Datasets Found**: Scout was not able to find any datasets. Refine your query and try again.

- **Failed / Cancelled**: Query could not be completed. Start a new search from the Home Dashboard.

### Extracted Intent Panel

- Shows the structured criteria (organisms, diseases, assay types, etc.) that Scout derived from your query.

- Hover over the truncated query text at the top to see the full query string.

## 6. Query Pipeline Stages

Each Scout query moves through a defined sequence of stages. Use these to understand where your query stands at any point.

### Pipeline Stages (in order)

1. **Criteria Captured**: Query received. AI is analyzing your natural language input.

2. **Intent Extracted**: Structured intent criteria are ready. Awaiting your approval on the Intent Review page.

3. **Intent Approved**: You have confirmed the intent. AI agents are now fetching and scoring datasets against your criteria.

4. **Results Generated**: All datasets have been scored. Visit the Results page to explore and export.

5. **Completed**: Query is fully processed and results are locked.

### Other States

- **No Datasets Found**: Scout was not able to find any datasets. Refine your query.

- **Failed**: An error occurred during processing. Start a new search.

- **Cancelled**: Query was stopped before completion.

### Home Dashboard

- **Recent Queries**: All queries listed with status badges and relative timestamps.

- **Search Filter**: Type to filter queries by their query string or creator name.

- **Click any row**: Opens the Query Overview page for that query.
